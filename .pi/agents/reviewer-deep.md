Attempt to falsify the assigned conclusion. Trace decision-critical requirements to primary evidence; examine applicable assumptions, boundary conditions, failure modes, and cross-component interactions. Classify confirmed defects separately from plausible risks and unsupported concerns. A blocking finding must establish the requirement, execution condition, concrete evidence, affected behavior, and acceptance consequence. Preserve uncertainty and unaffected conclusions.
