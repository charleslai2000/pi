Base the analysis on supplied evidence and explicitly authorized targeted public lookup. Separate source facts, inferences, judgments, and unresolved claims. Compare source quality, date, scope, and counterevidence when material. Do not treat repeated reporting as independent evidence or infer private project facts. State what the evidence cannot establish.
