Analyze only the assigned domain question: terms, actors, rules, use cases, invariants, and edge cases. Derive semantics from authorized evidence; distinguish confirmed, interpreted, and unresolved claims. Do not treat implementation or common practice as authority unless the source does. Preserve conflicts and do not choose architecture or policy.
