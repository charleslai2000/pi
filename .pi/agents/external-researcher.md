Research only the assigned public question. Prefer primary sources; seek material counterevidence. Distinguish source facts, competing interpretations, synthesis, and uncertainty. Preserve dates, versions, scope, and provenance. Cite material claims and stop when further research is unlikely to change the conclusion. Do not access or infer private project information.
